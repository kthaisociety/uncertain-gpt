\begin{abstract}
% The suite first, then what it reported on the arms. Not a mechanism claim:
% the instrument is the contribution and the four measurements are readings
% off it. Every number here is a cell in a generated table or a constant from
% the source.
uncertain-gpt is a benchmark suite for what a Bayesian deep learning method costs to
train. One CUDA implementation of a GPT-2 of $11{,}061{,}531$ parameters supplies the
kernels, the forward and backward pass, the dataloader, the checkpoint format, the
timing harness and the evaluation harness, and the AdamW constants, the batch order
and fp32 arithmetic are shared as well, so an arm differs from every other arm only in
what it stores and how many passes it makes over that store. A method enters as an
update kernel and a callback returning posterior draw $s$. Bayes-by-Backprop, Vadam, a
learned low-rank-plus-diagonal Gaussian covariance and a five-member deep ensemble are
the reference arms, each a method from published work. Across a $256$-fold batch sweep
at fixed sequence length, each arm's posterior work holds to within $1.5\%$ of itself
while tokens per step go from $64$ to $16{,}384$, so its share of the step falls
towards $1/B$ above $B \approx 16$ and more slowly below it, where the network's own
passes are launch-bound rather than token-bound. Folding an arm's parameter-sized
passes into one kernel cuts Bayes-by-Backprop's $O(P)$ time by $1.76\times$ and leaves
Vadam's inside the $1\%$ band the unchanged control arm reproduces itself in, which
Vadam's $11$ buffer touches per step against a floor of $9$ predicted before the sweep
ran. Vadam's fitted posterior at this scale is the prior it started from, and an
epistemic term is biased low until its sample count converges, at an $S$ that differs
by arm.
\end{abstract}
