\section{Four optimisation strategies over one network}
\label{sec:methods}

% Per the brief for this section: what each arm stores, how many parameter-sized
% passes a step makes over that store, and what that buys. Pass counts belong
% here; their measured cost belongs in Section~\ref{sec:cost}. The arms'
% mathematics is in the cited papers and in the repository, not restated here.

The shared header holds the kernels, buffer layout, forward and backward pass,
dataloader, checkpoint format and evaluation harness; each arm's source file holds only
its optimiser. Cost is counted there in \emph{touches}, one read or one write of a
parameter-sized buffer in a step, which at $P = 11{,}061{,}531$ parameters in fp32 is
$44.2$\,MB. A deterministic AdamW step costs eight: a gradient zeroed and read, weights
and two moments each read and written.

Each arm is counted twice below, as the passes the method needs and as the passes left
after the redundant ones are folded into one kernel. One observation does most of that
folding in all three Bayesian arms: a gradient accumulator for a variational parameter
is write-only, so it never has to reach memory and becomes a register, and the next
step's draw folds in with it as a function of the parameters the update just wrote. The
fold is gated as an implementation of the same arithmetic
(Section~\ref{sec:quality}).

\paragraph{Bayes-by-Backprop.}
A fully factorised Gaussian, drawn as $w_i = \mu_i +
\mathrm{softplus}(\rho_i)\,\varepsilon_i$ once per step for the whole batch and
reparameterised so the gradient reaches both moments
\citep{kingma2014autoencoding,blundell2015weight}, against a prior of standard
deviation $0.1$ under an ELBO whose KL weight is $1/N$ for $N$ the training token
count, read off the loader rather than tuned. It stores $\mu$, $\rho$ and an AdamW
moment pair for each, where a deterministic model stores weights and one pair, and
runs AdamW twice a step: eight launches and $32$ touches over ten buffers, the most
of the four. Folding leaves one launch and $15$ touches over eight, the floor its
state implies.

\paragraph{Vadam.}
Adam's second moment already estimates a curvature, so \citet{khan2018vadam} reuse
it as the posterior precision and allocate nothing for the posterior. The mean
alone is free and the scale is read off the optimiser,
\begin{equation}
  \sigma_i = \frac{1}{\sqrt{N s_i + \lambda}} ,
  \qquad
  w_i = \mu_i + \sigma_i \varepsilon_i ,
  \label{eq:vadam-sigma}
\end{equation}
with $\lambda = 1/\sigma_{\text{init}}^{2} = 2500$ the prior precision, the
initialisation rather than a tuned value, and the square root absent from $\sigma$
following that paper's footnote~2. Its update is Figure~1 of the same paper transcribed
unchanged, one Adam step with the prior folded into the gradient, so the state is $\mu$
with Adam's own $m$ and $s$: three launches and $11$ touches against a floor of $9$, two
touches of slack and no accumulator to delete.

\paragraph{Low-rank plus diagonal covariance.}
This arm buys correlation with memory. The covariance is $\Sigma = A A^{\top} +
\mathrm{diag}(d^{\,2})$ at rank $r = 8$, never formed, and a draw is $w_i = \mu_i +
\sum_{k<r} A_{ik} z_k + d_i \varepsilon_i$ with one $z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_r)$ per
weight matrix per forward pass, shared by all $P$ of its weights; that shared draw
is the coupling, and the KL reaches $\log\det \Sigma$ through an $r \times r$
determinant. The posterior covers the four GEMM weight matrices per block, $24$
tensors holding $10{,}616{,}832$ parameters. Counted per Bayesian layer in units of
that layer's parameter count, so that $A$ counts as $r$, it costs two launches per
layer plus four global, $122$ units at $r = 8$, folding to one launch per layer and
$58$. It is the one arm left above its floor: its KL setup goes through a cuBLAS
call per layer and a Cholesky on the host, so it cannot fold into the update it
feeds, needs a reduction over each layer rather than one index, and round-trips $r
\times r$ matrices through the host.

\paragraph{Deep ensemble (control).}
$E = 5$ deterministic models \citep{lakshminarayanan2017simple} differing only in
the initialisation seed, which with no dropout and a sequential loader is the only
source of diversity; member probabilities are averaged at inference, never logits.
The arm stores no posterior and introduces no kernels, so its step is the
deterministic two launches and eight touches, already at the floor. It is left
unfused on purpose: its in-training output is the reference the repository is
calibrated against and its code is identical in both sweeps, which makes it the
drift control in Section~\ref{sec:cost}. Its cost sits elsewhere, at $E$ times the
step, $E$ times the memory, $E$ training runs, and an evaluation-time sample count
capped at the members it paid to train.
