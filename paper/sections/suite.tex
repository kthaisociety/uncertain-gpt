\section{The suite}
\label{sec:suite}

\paragraph{What every arm shares.}
A GPT-2 \citep{radford2019language} of the usual pre-LayerNorm shape
\citep{vaswani2017attention} at $C = 384$, $6$ heads, $6$ blocks, $V = 27$, trained at
$T = 64$ and $B = 4$: $11{,}061{,}531$ parameters, the six blocks holding about
$96\%$. Attention is the IO-aware formulation of \citet{dao2022flashattention};
format, loader and reference implementation follow llm.c \citep{karpathy2024llmc} and
nanoGPT \citep{karpathy2022nanogpt}. The corpus is text8 \citep{mahoney2011text8},
character-level over $27$ symbols in the standard $90$M/$5$M/$5$M split, walked
sequentially so every arm sees the same tokens in the same order, for $100{,}000$
steps. One RTX 2060 Max-Q, \texttt{sm\_75}, $6$\,GB, fp32 throughout, the four GEMMs
per block through cuBLAS. AdamW \citep{kingma2015adam,loshchilov2019adamw} at learning
rate $10^{-4}$, $\beta = (0.9, 0.999)$, $\epsilon = 10^{-8}$, no weight decay, no
schedule, and Philox 4x32-10 \citep{salmon2011parallel} for randomness, so a
perturbation is a pure function of (seed, step, weight index), regenerated in the
backward pass rather than stored.

\paragraph{What an arm supplies.}
Two things: a kernel that updates whatever the method stores, and a
\texttt{uq\_sample\_fn}, a callback returning a device pointer to posterior draw $s$.
Roughly $150$ lines of CUDA in the reference arms, against a shared header of
$1{,}610$. Nothing else is an arm's to change. The buffer layout is one table both the
network and every arm index through, and an arm's file is compiled both as its own
binary and into a Python extension, so the kernels a reader times and the kernels a
reader imports are the same kernels. Section~\ref{sec:adding} states the three
conventions that requires.

\paragraph{Timing a region.}
Step times come from the real training step with CUDA event marks at region
boundaries, one region per kernel group, rather than from a copy of it that can drift,
and a region's name records whether its cost is $O(P)$ and Bayesian, $O(P)$ and paid
by a deterministic trainer too, or $O(B \cdot T)$. Warm-up is discarded and the
statistic is a median with $p25$ and $p75$ beside it, so a throttled bimodal sample
stays visible instead of smearing into a mean. Temperature and SM clock are probed
every $20$ steps and printed with each measurement, the binary aborts above
$89$\,\textdegree C, and the driver idles between points until the card is under
$72$\,\textdegree C. A point with less than $384$\,MB of VRAM headroom is reported out
of range rather than measured (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). The sweep runs $B = 1$
to $256$ at $T = 64$ held. Touch counts, one read or one write of a parameter-sized
buffer, are cross-checked rather than trusted: converting a region's time to achieved
bandwidth (Table~\ref{tab:bandwidth}) has to put structurally unrelated regions in one
band.

\paragraph{Measuring quality.}
Evaluation loads a checkpoint and never trains, and each arm supplies only the
callback returning draw $s$. Over $S$ draws per position, with predictive
distributions $p_s$ and mixture $\bar{p} = \frac{1}{S}\sum_s p_s$, the shared harness
reports negative log predictive density of mixture and single draw, expected
calibration error over $15$ bins of argmax confidence \citep{guo2017calibration}, and
the decomposition \citep{kendall2017uncertainties}
\begin{equation}
  \underbrace{H[\bar{p}]}_{\text{total}}
  \;=\;
  \underbrace{\tfrac{1}{S}\textstyle\sum_s H[p_s]}_{\text{aleatoric}}
  \;+\;
  \underbrace{\tfrac{1}{S}\textstyle\sum_s \mathrm{KL}(p_s \,\|\, \bar{p})}_{\text{epistemic}} ,
  \label{eq:decomp}
\end{equation}
in nats, in double precision, mixing samples as probabilities and never as logits.
The off-distribution stream is uniform-random token ids from a fixed seed.

\paragraph{What can be proved about a change to the suite.}
Three instruments, in increasing strength: an evaluation whose output is
byte-comparable across a change to the training path, a kernel-equivalence harness
that can demand exact bit equality by rebuilding with multiply-add contraction
disabled, and a trajectory diff against source rebuilt from an earlier commit.
Section~\ref{sec:verification} reports what they returned here.
