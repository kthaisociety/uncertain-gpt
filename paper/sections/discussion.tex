\label{sec:discussion}

The ensemble costs $E$ training runs and returns the best predictive quality
and the widest separation between in- and off-distribution input. The
variational arms buy their posterior inside one training run and return a
better-calibrated model whose epistemic term barely reacts to uniform noise.
Calibration and separation measure different failures.

The low-rank arm pays memory: rank $8$ costs eight covariance tensors over the
Bayesian parameter block, and buys correlation within a weight matrix and none
across matrices. Mean-field pays a second variational tensor and a second
optimiser pass. Vadam pays almost nothing and gets almost nothing, which
Section~\ref{sec:quality} traces to $N s$ against $\lambda$. The claim that a
posterior can be nearly free
\citep{khan2018vadam,osawa2019practical,shen2024ivon} is not contradicted by
that. The posterior was nearly free. It was also nearly the prior. Whether the
two can be separated at this gradient scale is open. The knob is $\lambda$,
which stayed at the initialisation value, because choosing it by anything else
is a modelling decision and not a measurement.

The estimator that reports epistemic uncertainty is itself Monte Carlo, and
under-sampling suppresses the in-distribution term more than the
off-distribution one, so a small $S$ makes a variational posterior look better
at separation than it is.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
Everything here runs from the checkpoints in the repository. No number required
a retraining run, and evaluation reproduces bit for bit. The same code is
packaged as a PyTorch extension that compiles the four arms into one
translation unit, implements no kernel, optimiser or metric of its own, and
reproduces the binaries' output exactly at a per-step overhead between
$-0.45\%$ and $+0.80\%$, inside run-to-run spread. Training does not reproduce
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).

\paragraph{What would change the picture.}
A sweep over each arm's injected noise scale, which runs against saved
checkpoints and would separate posterior quality from the amount of noise an
arm injects. A second text domain in place of the synthetic off-distribution
stream \citep{ovadia2019trust}. More than one seed per arm, which would turn
the small gaps in these tables into resolvable ones.
