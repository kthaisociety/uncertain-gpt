\section{Correctness, and what the cost buys}
\label{sec:quality}

% Short by design. First that folding did not change what the arms compute, then
% what the four posteriors return at a fixed step budget. Interpret, do not
% retype, and do not stage.

Folding is a claim about implementation, so it needs a gate a wrong fold fails.
Evaluation at $S = 32$ on validation and test is byte-identical before and after on all
four arms, and is the weakest of the three checks run: it draws with the unfused sample
kernels, so a broken training path passes it. Running each fused kernel and the
sequence it replaces on identical inputs leaves mean-field and Vadam bit-identical and
the low-rank arm within $1.00$ ULP, and rebuilding with multiply-add contraction
disabled makes all three bit-identical, which puts the residual in contraction rather
than in the arithmetic. And $100$ steps of
normal-mode training against the pre-folding source reproduce every printed step line
on all four arms, including both summary lines of the control.

What the cost buys is read from the $100{,}000$-step checkpoints, on both splits with
neither preferred. The ensemble has the best mixture NLPD and the worst ECE by close to
a factor of two and Vadam holds the opposite corner (Table~\ref{tab:quality});
mean-field and the low-rank arm sit between them, separated by less than the two splits
disagree about them. No ranking is claimed on the NLPD column: with one run per arm and
an \texttt{atomicAdd} backward pass, nothing here measures the noise floor its spread
would have to clear. It separates the arms by percent, the ECE, off-distribution and cost
separations by multiples. The ensemble also separates in- from off-distribution input by
more than twice the margin of any variational arm (Table~\ref{tab:ood}), whose
epistemic terms barely move on uniform-random token ids.

A larger epistemic term is not a better posterior. Vadam's in-distribution epistemic
is more than three times mean-field's (Table~\ref{tab:uncertainty}) while its
separation ratio sits with the other variational arms, because its spread is the
prior's: Equation~\ref{eq:vadam-sigma} weighs the data's contribution to the precision,
$N s$, against $\lambda = 2500$, so the per-token RMS gradient would have to reach
$\sqrt{\lambda / N} = 5.27 \times 10^{-3}$ where the median observed value is $2.71
\times 10^{-5}$.\footnote{Read from the trained checkpoint by
\texttt{dev/vadam\_posterior.py}, the one measurement here taken from a checkpoint
rather than a logged run.} Noise that does not shrink as the mean fits is also why that
arm fails the one-batch memorisation gate.

Every uncertainty number above carries its $S$, because the estimator is Monte Carlo
over $S$ draws and biased low until it converges
(Figure~\ref{fig:s-convergence}). Mean-field is flat by $S = 32$ and confirmed flat to
$S = 128$, though its ECE rises at every doubling over that range while its mixture
NLPD improves; the low-rank arm gains a few percent of epistemic between $S = 32$ and
$S = 64$, so whether the convention moves to $S = 64$ is open.
