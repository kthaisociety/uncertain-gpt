\section{Experimental setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Model.}
A GPT-2 \citep{radford2019language} of the usual pre-LayerNorm transformer
shape \citep{vaswani2017attention}: width $C = 384$, $6$ heads, $6$ blocks,
vocabulary $V = 27$, maximum context $1024$, trained at sequence length
$T = 64$ and batch $B = 4$. That is $11{,}061{,}531$ parameters, of which the
six transformer blocks hold about $96\%$. Attention uses the IO-aware
formulation of \citet{dao2022flashattention}, so no $(B, \mathrm{NH}, T, T)$
score buffer is allocated. Data format, loader and reference implementation
follow llm.c \citep{karpathy2024llmc} and nanoGPT \citep{karpathy2022nanogpt}.

\paragraph{Data.}
text8 \citep{mahoney2011text8}, character-level over $27$ symbols (lowercase
\texttt{a}--\texttt{z} plus space), in the standard split: the first $90$M
tokens for training, the next $5$M for validation, the last $5$M for test. The
loader walks the corpus sequentially and hands out $B \cdot T = 256$ contiguous
tokens per step, so every arm sees the same tokens in the same order. Each arm
trained for $100{,}000$ steps, or $25.6$M tokens.

\paragraph{Hardware and precision.}
One RTX 2060 Max-Q, compute capability \texttt{sm\_75}, $6$\,GB of VRAM, fp32
throughout. The four GEMMs per block go through cuBLAS; every other kernel is
hand-written, benchmarked in \texttt{dev/} and checked against the
single-threaded C reference.

\paragraph{Optimiser and RNG.}
AdamW \citep{kingma2015adam,loshchilov2019adamw} in every arm with the same
constants: learning rate $10^{-4}$, $\beta = (0.9, 0.999)$,
$\epsilon = 10^{-8}$, weight decay $0$, no schedule. Randomness comes from
Philox 4x32-10 \citep{salmon2011parallel}, so a perturbation is a pure function
of (seed, step, weight index). The backward pass regenerates the forward pass's
$\varepsilon$ from the index and no arm stores a perturbation buffer.
Evaluation draws come from a seed disjoint from every training stream.

\paragraph{Measuring cost.}
Step time is wall time around the complete step, taken with CUDA events on the
device that ran it, and covers the host-to-device copy, buffer clears, weight
sampling, forward, backward and parameter update. VRAM is the sum of an arm's
allocations, computed from the buffer layout rather than read from a driver
tool, and excludes allocator slack and context overhead.

\paragraph{Measuring quality.}
Evaluation loads a checkpoint and never trains. Each arm supplies only a
callback returning draw $s$, and the shared harness does the rest. Per
position, over $S$ draws with predictive distributions $p_s$ and mixture
$\bar{p} = \frac{1}{S}\sum_s p_s$, it reports negative log predictive density
of the mixture and of a single draw, expected calibration error over $15$ bins
of next-token argmax confidence \citep{guo2017calibration}, and the
decomposition \citep{kendall2017uncertainties}

\begin{equation}
  \underbrace{H[\bar{p}]}_{\text{total}}
  \;=\;
  \underbrace{\tfrac{1}{S}\textstyle\sum_s H[p_s]}_{\text{aleatoric}}
  \;+\;
  \underbrace{\tfrac{1}{S}\textstyle\sum_s \mathrm{KL}(p_s \,\|\, \bar{p})}_{\text{epistemic}} ,
  \label{eq:decomp}
\end{equation}

in nats, accumulated in double precision. Samples are mixed as probabilities,
never as logits: a logit average is a geometric mean and carries the wrong
uncertainty.

Equation~\ref{eq:decomp} is an identity for any $S \geq 1$ and any sampler, and
both halves of the right-hand side are computed directly rather than one from
the other, so asserting it tests the accumulation and not the posterior. It is
also normalisation-free: an injected unnormalised row and an injected stale
sample buffer both held it to $10^{-15}$. The harness asserts it at $10^{-5}$
for every arm and exits nonzero.

\paragraph{Splits and the off-distribution stream.}
Every reported number carries its split and its $S$. The off-distribution
stream is uniform-random token ids from a fixed seed, evaluated over the same
number of positions as the in-distribution stream. Epistemic uncertainty has to
rise on it, and the harness warns when it does not.
