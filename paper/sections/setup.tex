\section{Setup and measurement protocol}
\label{sec:setup}

% Model, data, hardware, and what a region time is: the headline claim is a
% per-region time, so the protocol has to be stated. Every configuration number
% must be traceable to the repo.

\paragraph{Model, data, hardware.}
A GPT-2 \citep{radford2019language} of the usual pre-LayerNorm shape
\citep{vaswani2017attention} at $C = 384$, $6$ heads, $6$ blocks, $V = 27$, trained at
$T = 64$ and $B = 4$: $11{,}061{,}531$ parameters, the six blocks holding about $96\%$.
Attention is the IO-aware formulation of \citet{dao2022flashattention}; format, loader
and reference implementation follow llm.c \citep{karpathy2024llmc} and nanoGPT
\citep{karpathy2022nanogpt}. The corpus is text8 \citep{mahoney2011text8},
character-level over $27$ symbols in the standard $90$M/$5$M/$5$M split, walked
sequentially so every arm sees the same tokens in the same order, for $100{,}000$
steps. One RTX 2060 Max-Q, \texttt{sm\_75}, $6$\,GB, fp32 throughout, the four GEMMs
per block through cuBLAS and every other kernel checked against a single-threaded C
reference. AdamW \citep{kingma2015adam,loshchilov2019adamw} everywhere at learning rate
$10^{-4}$, $\beta = (0.9, 0.999)$, $\epsilon = 10^{-8}$, no weight decay, no schedule,
and Philox 4x32-10 \citep{salmon2011parallel} for randomness, so a perturbation is a
pure function of (seed, step, weight index) and is regenerated in the backward pass
rather than stored.

\paragraph{Timing a region.}
Step times come from the real training step with CUDA event marks at region
boundaries, one region per kernel group, rather than from a copy of it that can drift,
and a region's name records whether its cost is $O(P)$ and Bayesian, $O(P)$ and paid by
a deterministic trainer too, or $O(B \cdot T)$. Warm-up is discarded, $30$ steps
falling to $10$ at the top of the sweep, and the statistic is a median over the
retained steps with $p25$ and $p75$ beside it, so a throttled bimodal sample stays
visible instead of smearing into a mean. Temperature and SM clock are probed
every $20$ steps and printed with each measurement, the binary aborts above
$89$\,\textdegree C, and the driver idles between points until the card is under
$72$\,\textdegree C. A point with less than $384$\,MB of VRAM headroom is reported out
of range rather than measured (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). The sweep runs $B = 1$
to $256$ at $T = 64$ held. Touch counts are cross-checked rather than trusted:
converting a region's time to achieved bandwidth (Table~\ref{tab:bandwidth}) has to put
structurally unrelated regions in one band, and a wrong count lands off it.

\paragraph{Measuring quality.}
Evaluation loads a checkpoint and never trains, and each arm supplies only a callback
returning draw $s$. Over $S$ draws per position, with predictive distributions $p_s$
and mixture $\bar{p} = \frac{1}{S}\sum_s p_s$, the shared harness reports negative log
predictive density of mixture and single draw, expected calibration error over $15$
bins of argmax confidence \citep{guo2017calibration}, and the decomposition
\citep{kendall2017uncertainties}
\begin{equation}
  \underbrace{H[\bar{p}]}_{\text{total}}
  \;=\;
  \underbrace{\tfrac{1}{S}\textstyle\sum_s H[p_s]}_{\text{aleatoric}}
  \;+\;
  \underbrace{\tfrac{1}{S}\textstyle\sum_s \mathrm{KL}(p_s \,\|\, \bar{p})}_{\text{epistemic}} ,
  \label{eq:decomp}
\end{equation}
in nats, in double precision, mixing samples as probabilities and never as logits.
Both halves of the right-hand side are computed directly rather than one from the other,
so the identity, asserted at $10^{-5}$, tests the accumulation and not the posterior.
The off-distribution stream is uniform-random token ids from a fixed seed.
