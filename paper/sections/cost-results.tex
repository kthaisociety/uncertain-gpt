\section{What the posterior costs}
\label{sec:cost}

% contain has not been measured yet.

The three posteriors price differently by an order of magnitude, and the
ordering follows the buffer count rather than the arithmetic.

Vadam is the cheapest posterior on both axes, and its step lands within a
millisecond of the ensemble member's --- a deterministic step with a sampling
kernel bolted on. Its posterior is the optimiser state the run had already
allocated, so the only thing it adds is the sampling kernel of
Equation~\ref{eq:vadam-sigma}. Mean-field costs what a second set of
variational parameters costs: another parameter-sized tensor, its AdamW moment
pair, and a second update pass. The low-rank arm is the outlier in every
column, and the reason is the factor $A$ --- at $r = 8$ that is eight tensors
the size of the Bayesian parameter block, read, written and sampled from every
step. Buying correlation is a memory decision before it is a compute one.

The ensemble's row is one member's. Its step is the deterministic step,
because that is exactly what it is, and its bill is $E$ times that step and
$E$ times the training run. Read against the Bayesian arms it inverts the
usual reading: the cheapest arm per step is the most expensive arm to train,
and the only one whose evaluation-time $S$ cannot be raised without paying
again.

Vadam is also the arm that fails the one-batch overfit gate, and it fails it
for a reason the cost table does not show and Section~\ref{sec:quality} does.
The gate demands a single batch be driven to near-zero loss. Vadam's injected
noise does not shrink as the mean fits, so the loss floors well above every
other arm's while the run itself is healthy. It is a symptom of where its
posterior sits, not of the optimiser.

These are lower bounds. The card has a cooling fault, the runs span two
sessions that did not throttle alike, and the low-rank arm's step time drifted
within a single run (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}). The gaps between arms
here are large enough to survive that; a difference of a few milliseconds
between two rows is not a result.
