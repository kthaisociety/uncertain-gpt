Weight uncertainty was priced as description length by \citet{hinton1993mdl},
made practical for real networks by \citet{graves2011practical}, and turned
into Bayes-by-Backprop by \citet{blundell2015weight} through the
reparameterisation of \citet{kingma2014autoencoding}; \citet{wen2018flipout}
reduced the cost of drawing a perturbation per example.
\citet{khan2018vadam} reuse Adam's second moment as a posterior precision,
\citet{osawa2019practical} scale natural-gradient variational inference to
ImageNet, and \citet{shen2024ivon} apply it at GPT-2 scale. Structured
covariances fitted after training include a Kronecker-factored Laplace
approximation at a trained point \citep{ritter2018scalable}, the same
approximation as a recipe \citep{daxberger2021laplace}, and a
low-rank-plus-diagonal factor collected from the SGD trajectory
\citep{maddox2019swag}, which is the covariance the third arm here learns
during training instead. Deep ensembles \citep{lakshminarayanan2017simple}
match or beat variational methods on the benchmarks of \citet{ovadia2019trust}
at $E$ times the training cost; MC dropout \citep{gal2016dropout} is the other
cheap baseline, and the network here has no dropout layers. Metrics follow
\citet{guo2017calibration} for calibration and \citet{kendall2017uncertainties}
for the aleatoric and epistemic split.
