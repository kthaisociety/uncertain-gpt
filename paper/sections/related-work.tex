\label{sec:related}

Weight uncertainty was priced as description length by \citet{hinton1993mdl}, made
practical for real networks by \citet{graves2011practical}, and turned into
Bayes-by-Backprop by \citet{blundell2015weight} through the reparameterisation of
\citet{kingma2014autoencoding}, which pays for a posterior with a second
parameter-sized tensor and a second optimiser state; \citet{wen2018flipout} move a
per-example perturbation into the activations. \citet{khan2018vadam} instead reuse
Adam's second moment as a posterior precision, \citet{osawa2019practical} scale
natural-gradient variational inference to ImageNet, and \citet{shen2024ivon} apply it
at GPT-2 scale. Covariances fitted after training move the cost out of the step: a
Kronecker-factored Laplace approximation at a trained point
\citep{ritter2018scalable}, the same approximation as a recipe
\citep{daxberger2021laplace}, and a low-rank-plus-diagonal factor from the SGD
trajectory \citep{maddox2019swag}, which is the covariance the third arm here learns
during training instead. Deep ensembles \citep{lakshminarayanan2017simple} keep the
step deterministic and pay $E$ training runs, and match or beat variational methods on
the benchmarks of \citet{ovadia2019trust}; MC dropout \citep{gal2016dropout} is the
other cheap baseline, and this network has no dropout layers. Metrics follow
\citet{guo2017calibration} and \citet{kendall2017uncertainties}.
